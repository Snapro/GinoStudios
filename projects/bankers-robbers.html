Bankers & Robbers Virtual Set Design

ClientBankers & Robbers
Year2023
ScopeVirtual Set Design & 3D Environment
CategoryArchitectural Visualisation

The Brief

Design a bespoke virtual broadcast set for Bankers & Robbers — a bold financial and lifestyle show — that could serve as a dynamic, photorealistic backdrop for studio recordings.

The Challenge

The set needed to communicate both the energy of finance and the lifestyle aspirations of the show's audience, while functioning practically as a broadcast background with clear sight lines and camera angles.

Our Approach

We designed a multi-level virtual environment featuring sleek architectural elements, neon accent lighting, and a city skyline backdrop. Every element was crafted for broadcast compatibility, with lighting setups matched to studio conditions.

A striking virtual set that elevated the show's visual production value, giving it the look and feel of a major international broadcast while remaining achievable within Zambia's production context.
