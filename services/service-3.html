Graphics Design & Print

Graphics Design & Print

Great graphic design is not just about looking good — it is about communicating immediately, consistently, and memorably. At Gino Studios, our graphics and print service covers everything from the foundational brand identity work that defines a company's visual language, to the campaign-specific marketing materials that carry that language into the world.

We work across both screen and print contexts, understanding the technical requirements of each. Digital assets are built for pixel-perfect clarity across resolutions. Print materials are set up with correct bleeds, colour profiles, and file specifications for professional output — so what you approve on screen is what you receive from the printer.

Our approach is always brand-led. Even when producing a one-off event banner, we design it as though the rest of your brand ecosystem depends on it — because visually inconsistent brands erode trust faster than almost anything else.

Our Process

01

Brand Discovery

For identity projects, we start with a discovery session — understanding your business, audience, competitors, and aspirations. For one-off collateral, we review existing brand assets and guidelines.

02

Concept Development

We develop initial design concepts exploring visual direction — typography, colour, layout, and imagery style — and present these for structured feedback before full execution.

03

Refinement

Based on your feedback, we refine the chosen direction through iterative rounds — sharpening every detail until the design is exactly right.

04

Artworking & Preparation

All final assets are prepared for their intended output — correct print specifications, digital export formats, or brand guidelines documentation.

05

Delivery

You receive fully organised, named, and layered source files alongside production-ready exports in all agreed formats.

Common Questions

I already have a logo — can you help with other materials?

Absolutely. We work within existing brand identities to produce collateral that aligns with your established visual language. Please share your brand guidelines or existing assets when briefing.

How many concepts do I receive?

For identity projects, we typically present 2–3 distinct concepts. For individual collateral pieces, we present 1 developed concept with revisions included. Approach can be discussed at project kick-off.

Do you prepare files for print?

Yes. All print materials are prepared to professional print specifications — correct document size, bleed, slug, crop marks, and CMYK colour profile. We can supply press-ready PDFs, editable source files, or both.

Can you design for large-format print like banners and event graphics?

Yes, and this is a particular strength — our trade fair work demands large-format precision. We produce artwork at correct scale with attention to viewing distance and printing method.
