Architectural Visualisation

Architectural Visualisation

Architectural visualisation is no longer a luxury reserved for large-scale construction firms — it is a strategic communication tool. At Gino Studios, we apply photorealistic 3D rendering to a remarkably wide range of applications: residential and commercial architecture, government and private trade fair stands, virtual broadcast sets, tourism facilities, and retail environments.

Our workflow begins with a deep dive into your brief. We study your spatial requirements, brand language, and audience context before modelling begins. This ensures that every visual we produce is not just technically accurate but genuinely persuasive — built to win approvals, attract investment, and guide production teams.

We work primarily in Blender and industry-standard pipelines, producing renders that balance physical accuracy with visual drama. Night renders, aerial perspectives, interior walkthroughs, and animated fly-throughs are all available depending on your project's needs.

Our Process

01

Brief & Discovery

We review your plans, reference materials, and objectives — asking the right questions to ensure every render delivers against your actual goals, not just the surface brief.

02

Concept Modelling

Our 3D team builds the initial model, establishing massing, proportions, and spatial relationships. We share grey-box previews early so you can redirect before detail work begins.

03

Material & Lighting

We apply textures, materials, and lighting setups that match your intended environment — daylight, artificial lighting, exterior conditions, or branded colour schemes.

04

Render & Refine

High-resolution renders are produced and shared for client review. Revisions are incorporated efficiently, with typical turnaround of 24–48 hours per revision round.

05

Final Delivery

You receive production-ready images in agreed formats — optimised for presentation decks, print materials, social media, or construction documentation as required.

Common Questions

What do you need from me to get started?

Floor plans, reference images, brand guidelines, and a brief describing the intended use of the renders — presentation, print, social, or production direction. The more context you give us, the sharper the output.

How long does a project take?

A typical trade fair stand visualisation takes 5–10 business days. Complex multi-angle architectural projects may take 2–4 weeks. Timelines are confirmed at project kick-off.

Can you work from a sketch or concept rather than full plans?

Yes. For conceptual projects, we can develop the 3D model collaboratively — iterating on spatial concepts as the design evolves. This is common in exhibition stand and virtual set projects.

Do you offer revisions?

Every project includes structured revision rounds. We share preview renders before final production to ensure alignment before high-resolution output.
