Motion Design

Motion Design

Motion design is the art of giving ideas movement. At Gino Studios, we produce animation across the full spectrum — from high-fidelity 3D product visualisations and brand identity animations to hand-drawn 2D explainers and broadcast-ready motion graphics.

We understand that motion is not just decoration. A well-crafted animation communicates complex ideas faster than any written explanation, triggers emotional responses that static imagery cannot, and performs measurably better across digital platforms.

Our motion team is fluent in both disciplines: the technical precision of 3D animation using Blender and Cinema 4D, and the expressive craft of 2D character animation and illustration. This breadth allows us to match the right visual language to your audience and your message.

Our Process

01

Concept & Storyboard

We develop a visual narrative for your animation — storyboarding key scenes, establishing the pacing and tone, and aligning on the motion language before production begins.

02

Style Development

For 3D projects, we develop the look and feel through grey-box animation and material tests. For 2D, we deliver illustrated style frames showing the visual world of your animation before full production.

03

Animation Production

Our animators bring the storyboard to life — building scenes, timing movements, and refining details until every frame serves the story.

04

Sound & Music

We advise on and can source royalty-free music and sound design that complements the animation's pacing and emotional register.

05

Final Delivery

Exported in your required format and resolution — MP4, MOV, broadcast specifications, or social-optimised variants. Transparent-background versions available for 2D overlays.

Common Questions

What information do I need to provide?

A brief describing the animation's purpose, target audience, key message, and any existing brand assets (logo, colour palette, fonts). Reference videos you admire are always helpful.

How long does a motion design project take?

A short brand animation (10–30 seconds) typically takes 1–2 weeks. Longer explainer videos or complex 3D animations may take 3–6 weeks depending on detail and revision needs.

Can you animate our existing brand assets?

Yes. We regularly animate existing logos, brand elements, and static graphics — breathing movement into assets you already own.

Do you produce audio as well?

We provide sound design consultation and can source licensed music tracks. For voiceover requirements, we can connect you with professional voice talent.
