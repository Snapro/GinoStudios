Photography & Videography

Photography & Videography

Photography and video are the most immediate tools a brand has for communicating authenticity. Where 3D renders show what could be, photography and videography show what is — and when executed well, they make audiences believe in both equally.

Our photographic and video production service covers the full range of commercial and brand content needs: corporate event coverage, product photography, executive portraits, brand films, social media content production, and promotional videos.

We bring professional equipment, lighting expertise, and a creative eye to every shoot — but more importantly, we bring an understanding of how the content will be used. A headshot for a LinkedIn profile demands different treatment than a product image for an e-commerce platform. We calibrate our approach accordingly.

Our Process

01

Pre-Production Planning

We discuss the shoot's purpose, outputs, locations, styling requirements, and shot list in detail before the day. Good pre-production is what separates a productive shoot from a chaotic one.

02

Production Day

We arrive prepared — with equipment checked, lighting planned, and a shot list agreed. We move efficiently while remaining creative and responsive to opportunities that arise on location.

03

Editing & Colour Grading

Images are culled, selected, retouched, and colour graded to a consistent, premium standard. Video content is edited, colour graded, and export-optimised for its intended platform.

04

Delivery

Final assets are delivered via a private gallery or file transfer link in agreed formats — web-optimised, print-ready, or broadcast specification as required.

Common Questions

Do you shoot on location or in a studio?

Both. We have the equipment for on-location shoots across Lusaka and Zambia, and can arrange studio space for controlled product or portrait sessions.

How soon after the shoot do we receive the files?

Edited photography is typically delivered within 5–7 business days. Video production timelines depend on edit complexity — a highlight reel may be 3–5 days; a brand film may take 2–3 weeks.

Do you offer packages or is everything priced individually?

We offer both. Common service combinations can be packaged for efficiency, and we also price individual shoots based on scope. Contact us for a tailored quote.

Can you produce content for social media platforms specifically?

Yes — we produce content optimised for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube, including vertical-format video and appropriately-sized graphics and images for each platform.
