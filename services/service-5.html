Web Design & Development

Web Design & Development

Your website is working for — or against — your brand every moment of every day. It is often the first and most lasting impression a potential client has of your business. At Gino Studios, we build websites that earn that trust immediately.

We design and develop custom websites with a clear priority hierarchy: performance first, then visual excellence, then ease of management. A beautiful site that loads slowly or breaks on mobile is worse than no site at all — so we engineer for speed and mobile experience from the very first line of code.

Our web work draws directly on our wider creative capabilities — photography, motion, and 3D — so your website can incorporate the full visual richness of your brand rather than relying on stock images and templated layouts. The result is a site that genuinely differentiates.

Our Process

01

Discovery & Architecture

We map out your site's goals, audience journey, page structure, and content requirements before any design begins. Structure drives design, not the other way around.

02

Design

We develop full visual mockups in Figma — desktop and mobile — working through your feedback until the design is exactly right. No surprises in development.

03

Development

We build using clean, semantic HTML/CSS/JS or the CMS platform best suited to your needs. Performance, accessibility, and mobile-first responsiveness are non-negotiable.

04

Content Integration

We integrate your copy, images, and other assets — and can advise on or produce content where gaps exist.

05

Launch & Support

We handle deployment, domain connection, and post-launch testing. Ongoing maintenance, updates, and support packages are available.

Common Questions

What platforms do you build on?

We build custom static HTML/CSS/JS sites, WordPress sites, and Webflow projects depending on your budget, technical comfort, and content management needs. We advise on the right choice at discovery.

Do you handle hosting?

We can recommend and help set up hosting solutions — including Netlify for static sites and managed WordPress hosting. Domain registration and management can also be handled on your behalf.

How long does a website project take?

A straightforward 5-page portfolio or business site typically takes 3–5 weeks from kick-off to launch. More complex builds with e-commerce or custom functionality take longer — timelines are confirmed at project start.

Can you redesign our existing website?

Yes. Redesigns are a large part of our web work. We audit your existing site before proposing the new approach — carrying forward what works and improving what doesn't.
