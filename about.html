Who We Are

We Make It Unforgettable

We are a creative studio that believes that everyone has the potential to create something amazing — it just starts with an idea. We exist to help brands leverage cutting-edge technologies and techniques for their brand development and marketing efforts.

With a focus on setting our clients apart from their competition, we have ventured through all media sectors to build a confident approach to capitalise on almost any creative project.

Our studio also stands as a platform for creatives to come and share their passion for creativity, and to find joy and fulfillment in the work that they do. Whether you are a professional artist or a beginner, we welcome you to join us on a journey of self-discovery and personal growth.

At Gino Studios, we believe the best work is born from a love of what you do — and we are here to help you find and grow that idea.

Our Foundation

What We Stand For

Three principles guide every project we take on — from the first brief to the final delivery.

Knowledge Above All

We invest in mastery. Our team continuously sharpens its skills across disciplines — 3D, photography, motion, design, and development — to deliver world-class results for every client, regardless of scale.

Client-First Approach

You create the narrative — we make it unforgettable. Every project begins with deep listening and ends with results that genuinely exceed expectations. Your vision is always the starting point.

Creative Community

We are a platform for creatives to share their passion and grow. We believe the best ideas emerge from collaboration — both within our studio and through our relationships with clients and partners.

Leadership

The Vision Behind the Studio

Founder & Creative Director

Joshua Bolton

The visionary behind Gino Studios, Joshua brings deep expertise across photography, 3D visualisation, and creative direction — leading a team of specialists to craft compelling stories for brands across Zambia and beyond.

His approach blends technical precision with an artist's sensibility — ensuring that every deliverable is not just correct, but genuinely beautiful. Under his direction, Gino Studios has built a reputation for quality that punches well above the weight of a Lusaka studio.

Joshua's belief that creativity should be accessible — to brands of every size and to individual artists finding their voice — is the philosophy that shapes everything Gino Studios does.
